## Projects

### Utopia

A personal productivity dashboard — todos, timesheet, notes, habits, calendar, email, and Google Drive in one place, with AI assistance built in. I designed and directed it, defined the features, and debugged it into a working product. I'm now polishing it toward an MVP launch.

![Utopia dashboard](/assets/img/utopia-dashboard.png)

**Problem:** My tasks, notes, habits, and calendar lived in five different apps, and nothing talked to each other. I wanted one dashboard that pulled it all together and could actually help me plan my day.

**Challenges:** This was my first real full-stack app. I had to learn how the front end, back end, and database fit together, wire up integrations like email and Google Drive, and track down bugs across the whole stack instead of a single file.

**Results:** A live, working dashboard at [littleutopia.net](http://littleutopia.net/) with todos, a timesheet, notes, habits, a Pomodoro timer, a reading list, and an AI chat — targeting a full MVP launch by August 2026.

**Tech:** Python
[Live Demo](http://littleutopia.net/) · [Demo Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDDa0_Ie7SI)

### [BilboBagginsPBA](https://github.com/sidpatil11/bilbobagginspba)

A website studying Bilbo Baggins from *The Hobbit*, built for a school project.

![The Hobbit — A Study of Bilbo Baggins](/assets/img/bilbo-hobbit.png)

**Problem:** The assignment was to present a character analysis in a way that was more engaging than a written report.

**Challenges:** Turning a book analysis into a clean, multi-section website — designing the layout, organizing the content into sections like Motivations, Philosophy, and the Big Five, and making it look polished.

**Results:** A live, illustrated site that walks through Bilbo's growth "from dormant hole-dweller to bold trailblazer," organized into clear themed sections.

**Tech:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
[Live Demo](https://bilbobagginspba.netlify.app/) · [Source](https://github.com/sidpatil11/bilbobagginspba)
